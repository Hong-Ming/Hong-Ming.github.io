Biography

Hong-Ming Chiu is an Electrical and Computer Engineering PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), working with Prof. Richard Y. Zhang. He received his Bachelor's Degree in Electronics Engineering from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Hsinchu, Taiwan (NYCU). He worked with Prof. Carrson C. Fung and Prof. Tian-Sheuan Chang during his undergraduate studies. He is a member of IEEE-Eta Kappa Nu, Mu Sigma Chapter and a former member of IEEE Student Branch at NYCU. His research interests include Machine Learning and Optimization.
